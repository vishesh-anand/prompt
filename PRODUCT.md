# PRODUCT.md

## What this app is

A daily speaking-practice app. You answer one question out loud, the app tells you what to fix, shows you a better version of your answer, and you try again. Over weeks, you get more fluent and confident when speaking without preparation, at work and in everyday conversation.

It is for people who know what they want to say, but it comes out messy when they have to say it on the spot.

## Goal for version 1

- Ready for 10 to 20 beta testers through TestFlight, 4 weeks after starting.
- iPhone only for now. Android comes later from the same code.
- **How we judge success:** do testers come back on day 3, day 7, and in week 3? If most stop by week 3, we rethink before building more.

## The daily practice

- **Daily minimum:** answer one question. That keeps your streak going.
- After that, you can keep practicing as much as you want.
- Each answer is **up to 60 seconds**. You can stop early.
- Before each question, you choose the kind: **Work** or **Everyday conversation**.

## Screens

### 1. Sign in
- Sign in with Apple only. One tap.

### 2. First-time setup
- Pick a reminder time (for example, 8pm).
- Answer your first question right away.
- That first answer is saved as your **starting point**, so you can compare against it later.

### 3. Home
- Choose Work or Everyday conversation.
- See today's question.
- See your streak.
- Big record button.

### 4. Recording
- Tap to start, tap to stop. Stops automatically at 60 seconds.
- A live sound wave while you speak, so you know it's recording.
- Answers under 10 seconds are not scored. Show a friendly "say a bit more" message.

### 5. Feedback (after each answer)
Shows, in this order:
1. **Filler words:** how many, and which ones (um, uh, like, basically, actually, you know, and "so" at the start of sentences).
2. **Speed:** too fast, good, or too slow.
3. **Clear point:** did the answer have a clear main point, a reason or example, and a proper ending?
4. **One tip:** the single most important thing to fix next. Only one.
5. **Better version:** your own answer, rewritten to be clearer and tighter.
6. **Replay button:** hear your recording. Optional, never required.
7. **"This feedback is wrong" button:** so testers can flag bad feedback.
8. **Try again button:** answer the same question again.

### 6. Before and after (after trying again)
- Your first and second attempts side by side: filler words, speed, clear point.
- Makes the improvement obvious.

### 7. Progress
- Streak (days in a row practiced).
- Weekly chart: filler words, speed, and clear point over time.
- Weekly note from the coach: what improved and what to work on next. Only shown after at least 3 practice days that week.
- Compare against your starting point.

### 8. Settings
- Change reminder time.
- Delete all my data.
- Sign out.

## Reminders
- One notification a day at the time you picked.
- **Only sent if you haven't practiced yet that day.**

## Questions
- About 100 hand-written questions: 50 Work, 50 Everyday conversation.
- No repeats until you've seen every question in that category.
- AI-generated questions are **not** in version 1. Planned for later.

## Limits
- Maximum 10 answers per person per day, to control costs.

## Privacy
- A short, plain note explaining that recordings and transcripts are stored to show your progress and improve the feedback.
- "Delete all my data" removes everything.

## Tracking (for the beta)
- Record only what's needed to answer: who came back, on which days, and how many answers they gave.
- Count how often "This feedback is wrong" is pressed.

## Not in version 1
- Payments or subscriptions
- Android
- Video, eye contact, or body language
- Tone of voice, accent, or grammar coaching
- AI-generated questions
- Social features, sharing, or leaderboards
- Any login other than Sign in with Apple
